## Style Guide for All Nrvana Studio Art

- **Ground:** Warm ivory (`#faf8f5`) or warm white (`#ffffff`)
- **Lines:** Single-weight, fine, dark charcoal (`#0d0d0d` or `#1c1c1c`)
- **Mood:** Architectural precision with a creative pulse. Deliberate. Unhurried.
- **No:** Gradients, fills, color, heavy strokes, decorative flourishes, text
- **Composition:** Process visible — construction marks, guide lines welcome
- **Reference feel:** Designer's sketchbook meets architectural drawing. Le Corbusier's notebooks crossed with a brand identity system.
